Full-text search across your cloud storage – private, offline, on-device.


FULL DESCRIPTION (max. 4,000 characters)
-----------------------------------------
Search all your cloud files with a single query – in seconds, without internet access, and without your data ever leaving your device.

FullTXT builds a local full-text index of your cloud documents directly on your phone. Once indexed, you'll find any file instantly – completely offline.

🔍 WHAT FULLTXT CAN DO

• Full-text search across all connected accounts simultaneously
• Search runs offline – no internet connection needed
• Results from 2 characters with highlighted text snippets
• Filter by file type, cloud provider and modification date
• Recent search terms are saved for quick access
• Open files directly from search results
• Automatically detect duplicates across multiple providers
• Light and dark theme – follow the system or choose your own

☁️ SUPPORTED SERVICES

Free: Nextcloud, ownCloud, MagentaCloud, Strato HiDrive, Yandex Disk, local folders
With FullTXT Pro: Google Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Dropbox

FullTXT Pro is a one-time in-app purchase – no subscription, no recurring fees.

📄 SUPPORTED FILE FORMATS

PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx),
OpenDocument (.odt, .ods, .odp),
Text files (.txt, .md, .csv, .log)

Files over 50 MB are skipped.

🔒 COMPLETELY PRIVATE

FullTXT has no proprietary server. The search index, your credentials and all file contents stay exclusively on your device:

• No analytics, no tracking, no third-party data sharing
• OAuth tokens encrypted in Android Keystore (AES-256)
• Temporarily downloaded files are deleted immediately
• Open source on GitHub

⚡ TECHNICAL HIGHLIGHTS

• Delta sync: after the first index run, only changes are downloaded
• Foreground service prevents Android from interrupting indexing
• Optional daily automatic background sync per account
• Index local folders on your device (e.g. subfolders of Downloads)

More information: fulltxt.me
